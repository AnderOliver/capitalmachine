Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can an available machine ship?

Shipping timelines vary by machine, location, and readiness. We’ll confirm the ship-ready date and discuss transportation and delivery requirements for the unit you’re considering.

How long will installation take?

Installation and commissioning timelines depend on the machine, its configuration, facility readiness, and project requirements. Capital Machine will work with you to establish an installation plan before you commit.

Can I finance an available machine?

Financing options and terms depend on the equipment and your circumstances. Contact Capital Machine to discuss your purchase and available financing arrangements.

Can I inspect the machine before purchasing it?

Yes. Depending on the machine’s location and availability, you can request a showroom visit, a live video walkthrough, or an on-site review with a sales engineer.

Does Capital Machine provide installation and operator training?

Yes. Installation, configuration, commissioning, and operator training can be coordinated as part of the equipment purchase. The scope and schedule will be confirmed for your specific machine.

What happens if I need parts or service after installation?

Capital Machine provides service support and access to OEM parts for the equipment it represents. Our team can also discuss a Preventative Maintenance agreement as part of your installation planning.

Is the equipment eligible for bonus depreciation?

Qualifying equipment may be eligible for bonus depreciation under applicable tax rules. Eligibility and the timing of any deduction depend on the specific purchase and tax circumstances. Confirm the requirements and projected in-service date with your Controller or CPA.
